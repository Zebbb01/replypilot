# Kettle & Pine store policies

Kettle & Pine is a fictional online store that sells coffee brewing gear. Each section
below is one policy the support agent can search and cite by its heading.

## Shipping times

Orders ship from our warehouse within 2 business days. Standard shipping takes 3 to 7
business days after dispatch; express shipping takes 1 to 3 business days. Every shipped
order has a tracking number, and the tracking link is in the shipping confirmation email.
We cannot change the delivery address once an order has shipped.

## Late or delayed deliveries

If tracking shows no movement for 5 business days, or the carrier marks a parcel as
delayed, we open a trace with the carrier. If the carrier cannot find the parcel within
10 business days of dispatch, we send a free replacement or refund the order in full,
whichever the customer prefers. Shipping fees are refunded for late express orders.

## Returns

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a
refund to the original payment method. The customer pays return shipping unless the item
is damaged or we sent the wrong item. Opened coffee beans and filters cannot be returned.
To start a return, the customer replies to their order email and we send a return label.

## Refunds

Refunds go to the original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the
returned item. Original shipping fees are not refunded, except for damaged, wrong or lost
items. We do not issue refunds as store credit unless the customer asks for it.

## Damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged or we sent the wrong item, the customer should send a photo of
the item and the packaging within 14 days of delivery. We then send a free replacement,
or a full refund including shipping if the item is out of stock. The customer does not
need to return a damaged item unless we ask for it.

## Cancelling or changing an order

An order can be cancelled or changed (items, size, or address) until it ships, for a full
refund. Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled, but it can be returned under the
returns policy after delivery.

## Warranty

Kettles and grinders carry a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects from the date
of delivery. Warranty claims need the order number and a short video or photo of the fault.
The warranty does not cover wear parts such as burrs and seals, or damage from misuse.

## Contact and response times

Support answers emails within 1 business day, Monday to Friday. We never ask for card
numbers or passwords by email. Chargebacks and legal matters are handled by a team lead.
